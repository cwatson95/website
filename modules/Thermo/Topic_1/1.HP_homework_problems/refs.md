## Methods / equations used
Weight `W = mg`; specific volume/density `v=V/m`, `ρ=1/v` (§1.5); mole count
`n=m/M`, `N=n·N_A`; hydrostatic/manometer `Δp=ρgL` (§1.6); absolute vs gauge
`p_abs=p_gauge+p_atm` (Eq. 1.14); temperature conversions (Eqs. 1.16–1.17);
`ΔKE`, `ΔPE` (Eqs. 2.5, 2.10); polytropic `∫p dV` work (Eq. 2.17 / Ex 2.1);
closed-system energy balance (Eq. 2.35b); steady-state rate balance (Eq. 2.37);
COP (Ch.10 defn, used here for the refrigerator). Canonical implementations in
module `1.EQ`; English-unit work uses `g_c = 32.174 lb·ft/(lbf·s²)`.
