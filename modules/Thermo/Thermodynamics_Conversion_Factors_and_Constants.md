# Conversion Factors & Constants

Reference values transcribed from the inside-cover tables of
**Moran, Shapiro, Boettner & Bailey — *Fundamentals of Engineering Thermodynamics*, 8th Edition**
(front matter, page 4 of `docs/thermodynamics`).

Notation: `·` is a multiplication (center) dot, `°R` = degrees Rankine, `lb` = pound-mass (lbm), `lbf` = pound-force.

---

## Constants

### Universal Gas Constant
- R̄ = 8.314 kJ/kmol·K
- R̄ = 1545 ft·lbf/lbmol·°R
- R̄ = 1.986 Btu/lbmol·°R

### Standard Acceleration of Gravity
- g = 9.80665 m/s²
- g = 32.174 ft/s²

### Standard Atmospheric Pressure
- 1 atm = 1.01325 bar
- 1 atm = 14.696 lbf/in.²
- 1 atm = 760 mm Hg = 29.92 in. Hg

### Temperature Relations
- T(°R) = 1.8 T(K)
- T(°C) = T(K) − 273.15
- T(°F) = T(°R) − 459.67
