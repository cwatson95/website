1. FOUNDATIONS & SYSTEM CONCEPTS
   - Open systems            — control volumes; mass & energy cross the boundary
   - Closed systems          — fixed mass / control mass
   - Extensive properties    — scale with amount (V, U, m, ...)
   - Intensive properties    — independent of amount (T, p, v, ...)
   - Quasiequilibrium        — idealized infinitely-slow process path

2. ENERGY & WORK
   - Work                    — energy transfer via a force through a displacement
   - Expansion work          — ∫p dV; the moving-boundary work of a gas
   - Compression work        — work input to compress; sign convention
   - Power                   — rate of energy transfer (work or heat) per time
   - Kinetic energy          — bulk-motion energy in the energy balance
   - Potential energy        — gravitational PE in the energy balance
   - Buoyancy                — ~CM (fluid statics); buoyant force, Archimedes

3. THE LAWS OF THERMODYNAMICS
   - Zeroth law              — thermal equilibrium & temperature
   - First law               — energy conservation; ΔE = Q − W
   - Second law              — entropy, directionality, Kelvin–Planck/Clausius
   - Third law               — entropy → 0 as T → 0 K

4. PROPERTIES & STATE FUNCTIONS
   - Enthalpy                — h = u + pv; the flow-work-inclusive energy
   - Entropy                 — s; the second-law state property
   - Exergy                  — availability; max useful work vs a dead state
   - Phase change            — latent heat; vaporization/melting/sublimation
   - Gibbs phase rule        — F = C − P + 2; degrees of freedom of a mixture

5. PROPERTY DATA: TABLES & DIAGRAMS
   - Saturation tables       — sat. liquid/vapor lines (-> steam_tables/ A-2,A-3,A-6)
   - Vapor tables            — superheated vapor (-> steam_tables/ A-4)
   - Liquid tables           — compressed/subcooled liquid (-> steam_tables/ A-5)
   - P-V diagram             — process paths & areas = work
   - T-S diagram             — process paths & areas = heat

6. PROCESSES & IDEALIZATIONS
   - Reversible processes    — no entropy generation; the limiting ideal
   - Irreversible processes  — real processes; entropy generation > 0
   - Adiabatic               — no heat transfer (Q = 0); isentropic if reversible
   - Steady-state            — control-volume properties constant in time
   - Conservation of mass flow — continuity; Σṁ_in = Σṁ_out at steady state

7. PERFORMANCE METRICS
   - Efficiency              — thermal efficiency, isentropic efficiency, COP

8. COMPONENTS & DEVICES
   - Compressor              — work-input device raising gas pressure
   - Condenser               — heat-rejection / phase-change exchanger
   - Heat exchanger          — two-stream thermal energy transfer
   - Heat pump               — reversed cycle for heating/refrigeration (see §9)

9. POWER & REFRIGERATION CYCLES
   - Carnot cycle            — the reversible benchmark; max efficiency
   - Otto cycle              — spark-ignition (gasoline) air-standard cycle
   - Diesel cycle            — compression-ignition air-standard cycle
   - Dual cycle              — combined constant-V/constant-p combustion model
   - Brayton cycle           — gas-turbine / jet cycle
   - Rankine cycle           — vapor power (steam) cycle (-> steam_tables/)

10. ENGINES (cycle realizations)
   - Carnot engine           — idealized reversible engine; Carnot efficiency
   - Stirling engine         — regenerative external-combustion engine

11. PSYCHROMETRICS (moist air)
   - Dry-bulb temperature    — ordinary air temperature
   - Wet-bulb temperature    — evaporative-cooling temperature; humidity link

12. COMBUSTION & REACTING MIXTURES
   - Fuels                   — heating values, stoichiometry, air–fuel ratio
   - Ionization              — ~PK; high-T dissociation/ionization (Saha)

13. COMPRESSIBLE FLOW & GAS DYNAMICS
   - Subsonic                — M < 1 flow regime; nozzle/diffuser behavior
   - Supersonic              — M > 1 regime; converging–diverging nozzles
   - Shock                   — normal/oblique shocks; property jumps
   - Laminar flow            — ~CM (fluids); ordered viscous flow
   - Turbulent flow          — ~CM (fluids); chaotic high-Re flow
