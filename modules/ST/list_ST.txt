  Probability Theory": 28 lessons, 5 sections -> 18 grouped modules below.)

  ST-01  Sample spaces & the axioms of probability -- set algebra, inclusion-exclusion   ~MA-19, ~QM-02
  ST-02  Counting techniques -- permutations, combinations, multinomial   ~SM-01
  ST-03  Conditional probability & independence -- multiplication rule, tree diagrams
  ST-04  Bayes' theorem -- law of total probability, posterior updating
  ST-05  Discrete random variables & expectation -- pmf/cdf, E[X], variance, moments   ~QM-06, ~SM-01
  ST-06  Moment-generating functions -- M(t)=E[e^{tX}], moments by differentiation, uniqueness   ~MA-10, ~SM-03
  ST-07  The binomial distribution -- Bernoulli trials, mean np, variance np(1-p)   ~ST-02
  ST-08  Geometric & negative binomial distributions -- discrete waiting times, memorylessness
  ST-09  The Poisson distribution -- law of rare events, Poisson process   ~QO-01, ~PK-04
  ST-10  Continuous random variables -- pdf/cdf, percentiles, the uniform distribution   ~MA-15
  ST-11  Exponential, gamma & chi-square distributions -- the gamma function   ~MA-12, ~SM-06
  ST-12  The normal distribution -- Gaussian, standardization, the Gaussian integral   ~SM-01, ~QM-07
  ST-13  Joint distributions of two random variables -- joint pmf/pdf, marginals, independence
  ST-14  Correlation & conditional distributions -- covariance, correlation, regression to the mean   ~MA-04
  ST-15  The bivariate normal distribution -- joint Gaussian, conditionals are Gaussian   ~MA-04
  ST-16  Transformations of random variables -- cdf & Jacobian methods, sums by convolution   ~MA-03, ~MA-09
  ST-17  The MGF technique & normal sampling distributions -- chi-square, Student t, F   ~ST-06
  ST-18  The central limit theorem & approximations -- normal approx, continuity correction   ~SM-01, ~PK-01
