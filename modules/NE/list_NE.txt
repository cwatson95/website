================================================================================
 [NE]  NUCLEAR SCIENCE & ENGINEERING
================================================================================
 shelf: books/library/NE_Nuclear_Engineering/ -- Shultis & Faw, *Fundamentals of
        Nuclear Science and Engineering*, 3rd ed. (CRC, 2017), with the authors'
        problem solution manual (nuclear_science_sol_shultis_faw.pdf).
        prereqs: QM-01..QM-08 (quantum basics), RE-06 (relativistic kinematics),
        ST-09 (Poisson statistics), Thermo-04..07, EM-06.
 (Seeded as a chapter-by-chapter replica of Shultis & Faw **Chapters 3-14**.
  Chapters 1-2 -- units/dimensions and the modern-physics review -- are
  deliberately skipped: that ground is already held by MA-01, RE-02..RE-06 and
  QM-01..QM-08, and repeating it here would duplicate three trunks. NE therefore
  opens where the book stops reviewing and starts doing nuclear physics.)

 Page convention: citations are to **printed** page numbers. In the PDF,
 PDF page = printed page + 23.

  NE-01  Atomic models -- Thomson, Rutherford, Bohr, the quantum atom   ~QM-03, ~QM-08
  NE-02  Nuclear models -- the chart of the nuclides, stability, liquid drop & SEMF, shell model   ~QM-14
  NE-03  Binding energy & separation energies -- the mass defect, B/A, the iron peak   ~NE-02
  NE-04  Nuclear reactions & Q-values -- conservation laws, exo/endothermic reactions   ~RE-06
  NE-05  Radioactive decay modes -- alpha, beta, positron, EC, IT, and their energetics   ~QM-22
  NE-06  Decay kinetics -- decay constant, half-life, mean life, activity   ~ST-11, ~MA-09
  NE-07  Decay chains & equilibria -- Bateman equations, secular equilibrium, radiodating   ~MA-11, ~PK-04
  NE-08  Binary reaction kinematics -- two-body kinematics, thresholds, the Coulomb barrier   ~CM-06, ~RE-06
  NE-09  Fission -- mass yields, prompt & delayed neutrons, the 200 MeV energy budget   ~NE-03
  NE-10  Fusion & nucleosynthesis -- the Gamow peak, stellar burning, the r- and s-processes   ~SM-08, ~PK-02
  NE-11  Attenuation & cross sections -- the linear coefficient, mean free path, flux density   ~ST-11, ~EM-15
  NE-12  Photon interactions -- photoelectric, Compton, pair production, mu/rho   ~QM-20, ~QO-02
  NE-13  Neutron interactions -- elastic scattering kinematics, lethargy, 1/v capture, resonances   ~NE-08
  NE-14  Charged-particle stopping -- Bethe stopping power, range, the Bragg peak   ~EM-09, ~PK-03
  NE-15  Radiation detectors -- gas-filled, scintillation, semiconductor   ~SM-12, ~QO-03
  NE-16  Counting statistics -- Poisson counting, propagated uncertainty, dead time   ~ST-09, ~ST-18
  NE-17  Dosimetry -- kerma, absorbed dose, equivalent & effective dose   ~NE-12
  NE-18  Radiation health effects -- deterministic & stochastic risk, LNT, protection standards   ~ST-12
  NE-19  The neutron life cycle -- the four- and six-factor formulas, k_eff, criticality   ~NE-13
  NE-20  Reactor kinetics -- point kinetics, delayed neutrons, reactivity feedback, Xe poisoning   ~MA-11, ~CM-15
  NE-21  Neutron diffusion theory -- Fick's law, the diffusion equation, criticality eigenvalues   ~MA-14, ~EM-03
  NE-22  Nuclear power reactors -- PWR/BWR steam cycles, Gen III & IV designs   ~Thermo-08, ~Thermo-09
  NE-23  The nuclear fuel cycle -- enrichment & SWU, burnup, spent fuel, waste   ~Thermo-11
  NE-24  Fusion reactors -- Lawson criterion, the triple product, MCF & ICF   ~PK-01, ~NE-10
  NE-25  Direct energy conversion -- thermoelectric, thermionic, AMTEC, betavoltaic, space power   ~Thermo-12, ~SM-11
  NE-26  Industrial & research applications -- tracers, radiography, NAA, particle accelerators   ~EM-09, ~NE-11
  NE-27  Medical applications -- projection imaging, CT, SPECT, PET, radiation therapy   ~NE-17, ~MA-09

 that need nuclear data read the CSVs above through `data_tables` (never by
 importing a sibling module).

 CONVENTIONS  (shared with the other trunks)

    README.md      scope, prerequisites, cross-links, the operations table, how to run
    notes.md       the derivations, each result tied to a code symbol
    refs.md        page-verified citations (printed page -> PDF page = printed + 23)
    code/          <topic>.py + test_<topic>.py, numpy + stdlib only
    problems/      worked problems with numeric *Check:* lines against the code
    figures/       make_figures.py -> SVG + captions.json
