# QO-05 — Squeezing & Nonclassical Light — Squeezed & Entangled Photons (notes)

A single field mode is one harmonic oscillator (`~QM-09`), and its vacuum is not
empty: the two field **quadratures** rattle with irreducible zero-point noise,
each with variance $\tfrac14$ — the **standard quantum limit** (SQL). *Squeezing*
is the trick of redistributing that noise: push one quadrature **below** $\tfrac14$
at the cost of inflating the other, keeping the uncertainty product at its
minimum. The states that do this — squeezed vacuum, and its two-mode EPR cousin —
are **nonclassical**: no classical stochastic field can reproduce their
statistics. They are the resource behind sub-shot-noise interferometry (LIGO),
continuous-variable entanglement, and the squeezed light of `~QO-06`'s $\chi^{(2)}$
parametric sources.

Citation key (full details in `refs.md`): **SZ** = Scully & Zubairy, *Quantum
Optics* (Cambridge, 1997). Cited at section/chapter level. Units $\hbar=1$.

## 1. Quadratures and the standard quantum limit
Write the mode's annihilation/creation operators $a,a^\dagger$ (`~QM-09`;
`annihilation`, `creation`) and split the field into two **Hermitian
quadratures** [SZ §2.7.1]:
$$X_1=\frac{a+a^\dagger}{2},\qquad X_2=\frac{a-a^\dagger}{2i},\qquad [X_1,X_2]=\frac{i}{2}.$$
The commutator forces a Heisenberg bound, saturated by the vacuum and every
coherent state (`~QO-01`):
$$\operatorname{Var}X_1\,\operatorname{Var}X_2\ \ge\ \frac{1}{16},\qquad
\langle 0|X_1^2|0\rangle=\langle 0|X_2^2|0\rangle=\frac14\ \ (\text{the SQL}).$$
Code: `quadrature_operators`, `quadrature_variances` (returns
$(\operatorname{Var}X_1,\operatorname{Var}X_2)$, both $=\tfrac14$ for
`vacuum_state` and `coherent_state`).

## 2. The squeeze operator
The **squeeze operator** is the unitary generated by photon *pairs* [SZ §2.7],
with squeeze parameter $\xi=r\,e^{i\phi}$:
$$\boxed{\ S(\xi)=\exp\!\Big[\tfrac12\big(\xi^{*}a^{2}-\xi\,a^{\dagger 2}\big)\Big]\ }.$$
Its generator is anti-Hermitian, so $S$ is unitary; `squeeze_operator(r, N, phi)`
builds it as `scipy.linalg.expm` of $\tfrac12(\xi^{*}a^2-\xi a^{\dagger2})$ in an
$N$-level Fock basis. Conjugating the ladder operators gives the **Bogoliubov
transformation**
$$S^{\dagger}(\xi)\,a\,S(\xi)=a\cosh r-a^{\dagger}e^{i\phi}\sinh r,$$
the linear mixing of $a$ and $a^\dagger$ that is the algebraic heart of squeezing
(and of parametric amplification, `~QO-06`).

## 3. Squeezed-vacuum quadrature variances
Apply $S$ to the vacuum to get the **squeezed vacuum** $|\xi\rangle=S(\xi)|0\rangle$
(`squeezed_vacuum`). For $\phi=0$ the transformation of §2 gives
$S^\dagger X_1 S=X_1e^{-r}$ and $S^\dagger X_2 S=X_2e^{+r}$, hence
$$\boxed{\ \operatorname{Var}X_1=\tfrac14 e^{-2r},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}X_2=\tfrac14 e^{+2r},\qquad
\operatorname{Var}X_1\,\operatorname{Var}X_2=\tfrac{1}{16}\ }.$$
One quadrature is squeezed **below** the vacuum noise $\tfrac14$ (the useful,
sub-SQL quadrature) while the conjugate one is anti-squeezed by exactly the
reciprocal factor — the product stays pinned at $\tfrac1{16}$, a **minimum-
uncertainty** state for every $r$. Tuning $\phi$ rotates *which* quadrature is
squeezed (code: `quadrature_variances` on `squeezed_vacuum(r, phi)`; $\phi=\pi$
swaps the two). The general (anti-)squeezed pair is $\tfrac14 e^{\mp2r}$.

## 4. Photon content — pairs, even-only, and $\langle n\rangle=\sinh^2 r$
Because $S$ is built from $a^2$ and $a^{\dagger2}$, it creates and destroys
photons **two at a time**. Starting from $|0\rangle$, the squeezed vacuum
therefore populates **only even** photon numbers [SZ §2.7]:
$$|\xi\rangle=\frac{1}{\sqrt{\cosh r}}\sum_{m=0}^{\infty}
\frac{\sqrt{(2m)!}}{2^{m}\,m!}\big(-e^{i\phi}\tanh r\big)^{m}\,|2m\rangle,
\qquad P(2m+1)=0.$$
The photon distribution and its mean follow:
$$P(2m)=\frac{1}{\cosh r}\,\frac{(2m)!}{\big(2^{m}m!\big)^{2}}\,(\tanh r)^{2m},
\qquad \boxed{\ \langle n\rangle=\langle\xi|a^\dagger a|\xi\rangle=\sinh^{2}r\ }.$$
Code: `photon_distribution` (odd-$n$ entries vanish to machine precision),
`mean_photon(r)` $=\sinh^2 r$ (closed form), and `mean_photon_number(state)`
(summed from $P$). At $r=0$, $S=\mathbb{1}$ and $|\xi\rangle\to|0\rangle$ — the
vacuum is the $r\to0$ limit.

## 5. Nonclassicality has several faces — $g^{(2)}(0)$ and the Mandel $Q$
"Nonclassical" is not one property. The photon-counting tests [SZ §4.4.4] are the
zero-delay second-order coherence and the **Mandel $Q$**:
$$g^{(2)}(0)=\frac{\langle a^\dagger a^\dagger a\,a\rangle}{\langle a^\dagger a\rangle^{2}}
=\frac{\langle n(n-1)\rangle}{\langle n\rangle^{2}},\qquad
Q=\frac{\operatorname{Var}n-\langle n\rangle}{\langle n\rangle}=\langle n\rangle\big(g^{(2)}(0)-1\big).$$
$g^{(2)}(0)<1$ (equivalently $Q<0$) is **sub-Poissonian / antibunched** light,
which *no* classical field can produce — a Fock state $|1\rangle$ gives
$g^{(2)}(0)=0,\ Q=-1$ (`g2_zero`, `mandel_q`). A coherent state gives
$g^{(2)}(0)=1,\ Q=0$ (Poissonian). The **squeezed vacuum is the cautionary case**:
it is profoundly nonclassical (quadrature noise below the SQL, even-only photons),
yet its photon number is **super**-Poissonian, $g^{(2)}(0)=3+1/\sinh^{2}r>1$.
So "squeezed" $\neq$ "antibunched": quadrature squeezing and sub-Poissonian
counting are *independent* nonclassical signatures.

## 6. Two-mode squeezing and EPR entanglement
Replace $a^2$ by the product $a\,b$ of two modes: the **two-mode squeeze
operator** $S_2(\xi)=\exp(\xi^{*}a b-\xi\,a^{\dagger}b^{\dagger})$ [SZ Ch. 16]
creates photons in *correlated pairs*, one in each mode, giving the **two-mode
squeezed vacuum** (`two_mode_squeezed_vacuum`)
$$|\mathrm{TMSV}\rangle=\frac{1}{\cosh r}\sum_{n=0}^{\infty}
\big(-e^{i\phi}\tanh r\big)^{n}\,|n,n\rangle,\qquad \langle n_a\rangle=\langle n_b\rangle=\sinh^{2}r .$$
It is **perfectly photon-number correlated** ($n_a=n_b$ always;
`two_mode_photon_distribution` is diagonal) and **entangled**. Its joint
quadratures beat the separable/vacuum bound — the continuous-variable form of the
EPR correlation (`~QM-21`, [SZ Ch. 18]):
$$\boxed{\ \operatorname{Var}(X_a+X_b)=\operatorname{Var}(Y_a-Y_b)=\tfrac12 e^{-2r}\ <\ \tfrac12\ },\qquad \tfrac12 e^{-2r}\to 0\ \text{ as }\ r\to\infty.$$
As $r\to\infty$ the position-sum and momentum-difference become perfectly defined
together — Einstein, Podolsky & Rosen's "simultaneous reality" of non-commuting
observables, realised by parametric down-conversion (`~QO-06`). Code:
`epr_variances`.
