## Notes (verified)
- Boas develops the binomial → Poisson (rare-event) and binomial → normal (de
  Moivre–Laplace) limits in §7–§9 (pp.756–769); §8 (p.761) is where the **normal as
  a limit** (the seed of the Central Limit Theorem) appears.
- §10 (p.770) is the experimental-measurement section that justifies the
  error-propagation and least-squares machinery — the daily statistics of a lab.
- Boas's "normal approximation to the binomial" is printed p.762 (within §8).
