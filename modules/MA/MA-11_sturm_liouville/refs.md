complete sets, orthogonality, eigenfunction series — in **Chapter 12** (and states
the Sturm–Liouville equation itself in the Ch.12 miscellaneous problems), with

## Notes (verified)
- The phrase "often called a Sturm-Liouville equation" appears in a Ch.12
  miscellaneous problem on **printed p.617** (the only in-body occurrence of
  "Sturm" outside the index). Boas's full *treatment* of the underlying ideas —
  completeness, weighted orthogonality, eigenfunction series — is §6–9
  (pp.575–582), demonstrated on the Legendre polynomials.
- `~MA-09` overlap is deliberate: the Fourier sine series (Ch.7) **is** the
  −y″=λy Sturm–Liouville problem of §3 in the notes.
