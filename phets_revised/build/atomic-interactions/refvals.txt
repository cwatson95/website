ANHARMONICITY.  Released at rest at r_min + A, the orbit runs between
r_in and r_out; its harmonic amplitude is a = (r_out - r_in)/2, which is
NOT A (the well is asymmetric, so the orbit's centre shifts outward).
Landau & Lifshitz Mechanics 28 for V = mu omega0^2 x^2/2 + mu alpha x^3/3
+ mu beta x^4/4 gives omega = omega0 + kappa a^2 with
kappa = 3 beta/(8 omega0) - 5 alpha^2/(12 omega0^3).  Here the Taylor
coefficients of the LJ well at its minimum are exactly
   V2 =    72 eps / r_min^2
   V3 = -1512 eps / r_min^3
   V4 = 26712 eps / r_min^4
so V4/V2 = 371/r_min^2 and (V3/V2)^2 = 441/r_min^2, and
   kappa = omega0 (371/16 - 2205/48) / r_min^2 = -(91/4) omega0/r_min^2,
   T(a) = T0 (1 + (91/4) a^2/r_min^2) + O(a^4).
